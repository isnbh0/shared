Before writing, rewriting, or reviewing Korean prose, read
`references/editorial-ko.md` in full. Treat it as editorial judgment, not a mechanical checklist.
Apply only the parts that fit the genre and preserve deliberate register choices supplied by the
user.

Produce prose that already follows the standards when drafting or revising. When reviewing,
identify patterns rather than isolated constructions, explain the concrete cost to meaning or
rhythm, and make or propose focused revisions. Preserve factual meaning and uncertainty. Do not
invent evidence, attribution, or specificity to make a sentence sound stronger.

If the reference file cannot be read, say so and do not claim compliance.
