# Sound production

Give each sound a job you can hear, then shape its attack, body, tail, spectrum, and place in the mix. The [audio synthesis guide](../../video-knowhow-code/knowledge/Audio%20synthesis.md) covers the signal and API details. This page connects those controls to how a component is executed and to the direction of the piece, and [musical development](Musical%20development.md) covers the notes and phrases.

## Shape and separate roles

| Control | Contextual use and interaction | Failure to inspect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Envelope | A short attack can mark contact; a body supplies duration and weight; a release can carry continuity. Schedule the perceptually important moment against the visual arrival. Edges: [shaped fades](../../video-knowhow-code/knowledge/Mastering%20and%20delivery.md#shaped-fades-the-pre-drop-gap-and-the-end-release). | A cue starts on time but blooms after the visible sync point; a long tail masks the next phrase. |
| Timbre and synthesis | Choose tone, noise, recorded material, and changing filter behavior for the action. The AX DAY reconstruction's `supersaw()` stacks detuned saw voices and opens the reveal chord's filter. Recipes: [sound sources and voices](../../video-knowhow-code/knowledge/Sound%20sources%20and%20voices.md). | Different actions share the same spectral and envelope recipe, so they lose audible distinction. |
| Chorus and detuning | Moving pitch or delay relationships can widen or animate a sustained source. The SLASH_MY writing notes describe chorus on the e-piano chords while the top voice stays dry. | The melody becomes diffuse along with its accompaniment; widening undermines the intended foreground. |
| Distortion and saturation | Nonlinear processing changes body and harmonic content as level changes. Inspect the cue after it enters the full bus. Mechanisms: [low-band drum saturation](../../video-knowhow-code/knowledge/Dynamics%20and%20spectral%20carving.md#low-band-drum-saturation-through-a-crossover), [soft clip before the limiter](../../video-knowhow-code/knowledge/Mastering%20and%20delivery.md#oversampled-soft-clip-before-a-true-peak-limiter). | The summed impact grows harsher as it gets louder, with no gain in force; a cue that was clean alone breaks up in the mix. |
| EQ and masking | Reduce competing energy where an important cue needs to read. The SLASH_MY reveal notes describe short spectral reductions around strikes and vocal words. The session records, held in John's workspace, log this rule as a recorded revision: when review found slams buried by the lead-in sounds before them, the decision was to turn the lead-ins down about 3 dB, and raising the slams was rejected. Mechanisms: [dynamic band carves](../../video-knowhow-code/knowledge/Dynamics%20and%20spectral%20carving.md#dynamic-band-carves), [strike carves](../../video-knowhow-code/knowledge/Dynamics%20and%20spectral%20carving.md#strike-carves). | Brightening every source increases competition; cuts remove the body that made a cue recognizable. |
| Compression and ducking | Control changing level and make space for a foreground event. Attack, release, and routing determine which parts actually step back. Mechanisms: [kick-keyed ducking](../../video-knowhow-code/knowledge/Dynamics%20and%20spectral%20carving.md#kick-keyed-ducking), [drum glue](../../video-knowhow-code/knowledge/Dynamics%20and%20spectral%20carving.md#drum-glue-compression), [bus compression](../../video-knowhow-code/knowledge/Mastering%20and%20delivery.md#light-bus-compression), [ducking under speech](../../video-knowhow-code/knowledge/Music%20under%20voice-over.md#ducking-under-speech). | The bass attack vanishes under a coincident kick, or the return stays loud after the dry signal ducks. Under narration, the music swells in short pauses or buries the first syllable. |
| Space | Per-source sends, delay, and reverb can give cues a shared environment and retain a dry foreground. The AX DAY reconstruction uses comb and all-pass stages with different left/right delay lengths. Mechanisms: [space and delay](../../video-knowhow-code/knowledge/Space%20and%20delay.md). | Every transient acquires a long tail; short feedback delays are treated as interchangeable with a diffuse room. |
| Mix hierarchy | Choose the foreground for each passage: speech, hook, action cue, or silence. Rebalance when that role changes. Under speech, set the music's level from the voice: [music under voice-over](../../video-knowhow-code/knowledge/Music%20under%20voice-over.md). | Every layer is individually audible but the viewer cannot tell what matters. |

These are proposed questions to start a listening review with, to adapt as the piece needs. They come from [two soundtracks](../cases/Two%20soundtracks.md), from the reconstruction's sound analysis and the C comparison (both summarized in [the comparison runs](../cases/Comparison%20runs.md)), and from the [SLASH_MY production notes](../cases/SLASH_MY.md).

## One impact inside a scene

The [title in the AX DAY reconstruction](Typographic%20events.md) puts a kick, a low impact, noise, and a sustained chord at the drop. The arpeggio and the later chime do other jobs: the arpeggio keeps things moving while the title holds, and the chime accents the sheen. Giving every subtitle a boom of similar size would weaken that hierarchy. In a restrained explainer, one quiet selection cue may express the event more accurately.

The silence before the drop is also a routing decision. The AX DAY reconstruction's master gate acts after the wet return is added. Muting only the dry voices would leave a tail in the gap. Its reverb keeps running under the gate, so the build's tail comes back with the drop. SLASH_MY clears its delays and reverbs at the gap ([comb and all-pass reverb](../../video-knowhow-code/knowledge/Space%20and%20delay.md#comb-and-all-pass-reverb)). A [continuity handoff](../patterns/Continuity%20carriers.md), on the other hand, may keep that tail across a picture cut on purpose.

## Review sound and delivery separately

Listen to the full mix, solo one role to find a problem, then go back to the full mix. Match the listening loudness when comparing versions: the AX DAY reconstruction and C files measured at different levels, which confounds any preference between them. Measurements can confirm peaks, silence, and that the encoded stream is present; timbre and musicality are judged by listening.

Use [audiovisual synchronization](../../video-knowhow-code/knowledge/Audiovisual%20synchronization.md) for clock mapping, [mastering and delivery](../../video-knowhow-code/knowledge/Mastering%20and%20delivery.md) for loudness, true peak, and measuring the muxed file, and [delivery and color](../../video-knowhow-code/knowledge/Delivery%20and%20color.md) for inspecting the delivered file. Each case's loudness targets come from its own delivery contract. Inspect the beginning of the delivered encode, the major collisions between cues, the quiet passages, and the ending. Measure the encoded file itself as well as the source waveform.
