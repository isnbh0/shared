# Diagnose a weak passage

Describe what fails at a particular moment before you choose a treatment. The same symptom can come from composition, motion, sound, or the surrounding sequence. The table lists candidate causes and proposed inspections to try.

| Observed weakness | Plausible causes | Alternatives to compare | Inspection question |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Unreadable | Last letters arrive too late; camera crop clips the phrase; surface competes with glyphs | [Shorten the stagger or preserve a stable text region](../knowledge/Typographic%20events.md); shorten copy; delay the next accent | At delivery size, when is the entire phrase first stable, and how long until attention moves? |
| Repetitive | Each scene has the same composition, semantic action, and accent schedule | [Vary mechanisms while retaining purpose](../knowledge/Direction%20and%20critique.md); develop a [motif](../patterns/Motif%20development.md); change density | Does each scene add a different fact or relationship? Does its motion have a different job? |
| Disconnected | Object identity, camera frame, or musical phrase resets unintentionally | [Carry one meaningful element](../patterns/Continuity%20carriers.md); use an explicit cut when the subject changes | Can you trace the same item through the boundary? Does the sound handoff help? |
| Weightless | Equal-time motion spacing lacks an intended acceleration; recoil has no contact; impact sound has no body | [Tune arrival and settling](../knowledge/Motion%20character.md); align cue body and contact in [sound production](../knowledge/Sound%20production.md); choose a deliberately light movement | Does the object appear to respond to something, or merely wiggle after arrival? |
| Thin or unfinished | Broken contour joins; ambiguous occlusion; weak scale; repeated lines without a clear form; unsuitable underlying concept | [Inspect form before surface](../knowledge/Form%20material%20and%20depth.md); compare a silhouette, repaired contour, and clearer recess; reconsider the object | Which visible relationship improves? Is the repaired form itself worth looking at? |
| Noisy | Every layer moves or sounds on every beat; grain or glow overwhelms the subject | [Reduce competing events](../patterns/Density%20and%20rest.md); restrict texture to a surface; separate sound roles | After removing one layer, is meaning lost or easier to follow? |
| Emotionally flat | The promise is vague; each act stays at the same intensity; reveal supplies no new consequence | [Revisit setup and payoff](../patterns/Meaningful%20reveal.md), [direction](../knowledge/Direction%20and%20critique.md), and [musical development](../knowledge/Musical%20development.md) | What does the ending answer, and what changed in the viewer's expectation? |
| Music feels unsteady | Intended syncopation is unclear; competing attacks imply different pulses; a cue collides with the score | [Inspect phrase and metrical placement](../knowledge/Musical%20development.md), then audition a narrow timing change such as [swing or a late snare](../../video-knowhow-code/knowledge/Groove%20and%20feel.md) | Can the listener follow the intended pulse? Does a repair help in the full phrase? |
| Sound feels small or generic | Cue envelopes and spectra resemble each other; attacks are masked; shared space is absent or excessive | [Change one cue's articulation or mix role](../knowledge/Sound%20production.md), keeping notes fixed for comparison; find the choice to revert or add on the [two-soundtrack ladder](../cases/Two%20soundtracks.md) | At matched listening level, does the cue better express the action's scale? |
| Narration hard to follow | The music sits too close to the voice; its melody, vocals, or swells land on words; low-pitched backgrounds mask speech for older listeners | [Lower the bed, duck it, or carve room for the voice](../../video-knowhow-code/knowledge/Music%20under%20voice-over.md); thin or drop the music under each line | Measured on separate stems, how far below the voice does the music sit? Can a listener with weaker hearing follow every word? |
| A warning card's claim doesn't match the film | The card names a time or place from an older flash check; the picture changed after it was written | [Take the claim from the latest flash check, or drop the timing line](../../video-knowhow-code/knowledge/Accessibility%20and%20perception.md#flash-warnings-that-follow-the-flash-check); inspect the failing frames before trusting the count | Do the times the card names match the latest run on the delivered file? In each worst window, does light change over a large area, or does only detail move? |

## Choose evidence that can answer the question

A still can show clipped text or a broken join, and the frames around a boundary can show a spatial reset. Continuous playback tests pacing and reading time, and listening tests groove, cue character, and mix hierarchy. A source audit explains how a passage was implemented, but it cannot replace any of those judgments of what is seen and heard. [The workbench](../knowledge/Production%20workbench.md) lists the matching checks.

The [comparison runs](../cases/Comparison%20runs.md) keep their negative findings. In D-007, the immediate and delayed word arrivals differed in their exact frames on the beat, but John found the difference hard to tell and not meaningful at playback speed. In C2, removing an extra kick at 7.81 s left the rest of the score unchanged, and John rated both blind excerpts bad. Once you have a diagnosis, the rounds that follow are run by [convergence](../knowledge/Convergence.md): what a review may reopen, what stays frozen, and when the fixing stops.
