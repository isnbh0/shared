# Read across scopes

Pick the scope that matches the decision you need to make. Recipes and cases are kinds of page that demonstrate these scopes. They are not a harder tier above them.

| Scope | Substantive route | Question to bring to review |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Foundations | [Attention, time, and meaning](../knowledge/Foundations.md), with static-design grounding — relational visual weight, grouping, and typographic roles — inlined where this shelf uses it | What must the viewer notice, understand, and retain? |
| Movement, appearance, and sound | [Motion character](../knowledge/Motion%20character.md) → [typographic events](../knowledge/Typographic%20events.md) → [form, material, and depth](../knowledge/Form%20material%20and%20depth.md); [sound production](../knowledge/Sound%20production.md) | Does the interaction of motion spacing, timing, surface, and cue character support this event? |
| Scene construction | [Meaningful reveal](../patterns/Meaningful%20reveal.md), [focused comparison](../patterns/Focused%20comparison.md), [defect to repair](../patterns/Defect%20to%20repair.md) | What changes in understanding when the scene completes? |
| Sequence composition | [Continuity carriers](../patterns/Continuity%20carriers.md) → [motif development](../patterns/Motif%20development.md) → [density and rest](../patterns/Density%20and%20rest.md); [musical development](../knowledge/Musical%20development.md) | What persists, develops, or provides relief between scenes? |
| Whole-piece direction | [Direction and critique](../knowledge/Direction%20and%20critique.md), with [the SLASH_MY case](../cases/SLASH_MY.md) | Do the premise, visual language, emotional arc, and ending belong together? |
| Production workbench | [Production workbench](../knowledge/Production%20workbench.md) → [inspect and deliver](../../video-knowhow-code/maps/Inspect%20and%20deliver.md) | Can the intended states and soundtrack be reproduced and checked in delivery? |

## Representative paths

**First component**: moving-text legibility in [foundations](../knowledge/Foundations.md) → [typographic event](../knowledge/Typographic%20events.md) → [AX DAY reconstruction title](../cases/Anticipation%20to%20title.md) → [direction](../knowledge/Direction%20and%20critique.md). Return to the implementation after deciding what the title must answer.

**First sequence**: [defect to repair](../patterns/Defect%20to%20repair.md) → [continuity](../patterns/Continuity%20carriers.md) → [density and rest](../patterns/Density%20and%20rest.md) → [musical development](../knowledge/Musical%20development.md). Inspect whether each repair has a different mechanism even when the strike recurs.

**First export**: choose a [representation](../../video-knowhow-code/maps/Choose%20a%20visual%20representation.md) → define shared events in the [workbench](../knowledge/Production%20workbench.md) → review frames, playback, and listening separately.

The [Operations](../../video-knowhow-code/Operations.md) remain available across these scopes. Source and layout operations serve components, event and correspondence operations serve scenes and sequences, and sampling and comparison operations serve review at every scope.
