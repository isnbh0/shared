# Integration ownership

The craft shelf owns the craft explanations that span scopes and the routes readers take through the package. Focused explanations, source records, implementations, and production documents stay where they already are. The table below names, for each subject that several pages touch, the page that explains it and how the others route to it.

## Inventory boundary

The reference collections this shelf draws on stay in John's workspace. The motion reference mirrors the public [Awesome AI Motion](https://github.com/gongnyang/awesome-ai-motion) repository at a recorded snapshot: about 30 focused motion decision notes, seven editorial maps, 15 generated family maps, 637 generated technique cards, 11 "timed recipes", seven deliverable routes, and a source glossary. The code shelf contributes the theory pages, knowledge pages, maps, recipes, operations, cases, and ledgers linked throughout. SLASH_MY is a read-only production source in that workspace; the SLASH_MY case records the inspected snapshot in prose.

## Consolidation and open boundaries

Pages in overlapping rows answer different questions or go into different detail. They link to each other, keep their own guidance and attribution, and no explanation is written twice.

This shelf works on the open gap between a production skeleton and convincing execution. SLASH_MY is the strong overall reference John endorses, and each of its recorded choices carries the case value this shelf attributes to it, so an adaptation can start from the evidence behind each choice.
