# Attribution

Personal notes on where things come from, kept with the package.

**The writing.** These two shelves are John's own practice notes: the craft generalizations, the case readings, the sample generalizations, and the starter. SLASH_MY is John's own production, and the samples and measurements quoted from it come from that production's records at the snapshots the pages name.

**Upstream card claims.** The motion reference the craft shelf draws on is a mirror of [gongnyang's Awesome AI Motion](https://github.com/gongnyang/awesome-ai-motion) at a recorded upstream snapshot. The mirror, in John's workspace, keeps per-card attribution and the upstream's own notices. Technique-card ideas, names, parameters, and "recipe" arrangements belong to that collection; where a page here leans on one of its cards, the relied-on claim is inlined and the collection is named. Static-design grounding comes from John's own design collection.

**Quoted sources.** External claims (standards, papers, vendor guidance) are credited inline where cited, collected with status and quotes in the [audio source ledger](video-knowhow-code/sources/Audio%20sources.md), and copied verbatim into [sources/excerpts/](video-knowhow-code/sources/excerpts/README.md) with their URLs and access dates, so the package keeps them when a link dies.

**Chatlog.** The earlier production's chatlog is John's own session record; the [chatlog index](video-knowhow-code/sources/Chatlog%20index.md) cites it by line and UUID.
