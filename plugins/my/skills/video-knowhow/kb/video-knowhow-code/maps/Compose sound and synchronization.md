# Compose sound and synchronization

Sound can carry the scene's dramatic change. In the [Stage intro](../cases/Stage%20intro.md), the recorded score builds toward a 0.125-second silence before the particle title. In [FOXTALE](../cases/FOXTALE.md), the recorded score moves through exploration, encounter, battle, mercy, and reprise, and the mercy choice stops the battle music and gives way to a softer passage. The chatlog records how the music was composed and timed. The assistant could measure levels but could not listen to the music. [Chatlog C-1549](../sources/Chatlog%20index.md#c-1549).

For each act, choose what the sound should do: a recurring theme, a new texture, a sudden stop, a cue, or silence. [Audio synthesis](../knowledge/Audio%20synthesis.md) explains how to make sound from code or samples. [Audiovisual synchronization](../knowledge/Audiovisual%20synchronization.md) explains how clocks and event times keep sound and picture in the relation you planned, in playback and in export.

To make a synthesized score sound produced, read the six technique pages below. They hold how two productions did it and the values each case used. One workable order, since each builds on the one before:

1. [Sound sources and voices](../knowledge/Sound%20sources%20and%20voices.md): what each part is made of, before anything is mixed.
2. [Groove and feel](../knowledge/Groove%20and%20feel.md): when the parts play, and how far to move them off the grid.
3. [Dynamics and spectral carving](../knowledge/Dynamics%20and%20spectral%20carving.md): how the parts make room for each other in level and frequency.
4. [Space and delay](../knowledge/Space%20and%20delay.md): where the parts sit in depth and width.
5. [Mastering and delivery](../knowledge/Mastering%20and%20delivery.md): what happens on the mix bus and in the delivered file.
6. [Music under voice-over](../knowledge/Music%20under%20voice-over.md): how to sit the finished bed under narration. Neither production has speech, so this page relies on broadcast research.

The [Soundtrack code case](../cases/Soundtrack%20code.md) holds the line references, parameter tables, and measurements behind those pages. [Two soundtracks](../../video-knowhow-craft/cases/Two%20soundtracks.md) compares the two scores side by side, and the [audio source ledger](../sources/Audio%20sources.md) records the external support.

For each important event, decide what in the soundtrack it lines up with: a beat, a note, a pause, a word, or a specific time. Then check the result in playback, because a schedule that looks right does not prove that sound and picture feel connected. [Time and motion](../knowledge/Time%20and%20motion.md) and [transitions and continuity](../knowledge/Transitions%20and%20continuity.md) cover the picture side of the handoff.
