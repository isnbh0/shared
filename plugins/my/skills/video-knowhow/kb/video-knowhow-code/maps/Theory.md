# Theory

These concepts help explain why a video communicates the way it does. Use them to form a guess about the picture before you choose effects or software. Each link leads to a short page with its own evidence and limits.

- [Attention and hierarchy](../theory/Attention%20and%20hierarchy.md): what draws the eye, and what should be understood first?
- [Grouping and continuity](../theory/Grouping%20and%20continuity.md): which elements belong together as the image and the scene change?
- [Temporal visual rhythm](../theory/Temporal%20visual%20rhythm.md): how do duration, repetition, and contrast shape what the viewer expects?
- [Moving-text legibility](../theory/Moving-text%20legibility.md): how much time and stability does reading need?
- [Visual identity and style](../theory/Visual%20identity%20and%20style.md): which recurring choices make a sequence feel coherent?

[Operations](../Operations.md) turns an explanation into an operation you can edit. [Techniques and approaches](Techniques%20and%20approaches.md) helps you choose a medium and inspect the result, and it also routes to the technical pages on audio production, capture, encoding, and delivery.
