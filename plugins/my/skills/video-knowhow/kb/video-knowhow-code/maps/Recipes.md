# Recipes

A recipe puts several capabilities together to produce one kind of piece. The [recipes shelf](../recipes/README.md) collects combinations backed by working examples you can inspect. Each recipe names the capabilities it uses and links to a result you can watch and to its source. Reuse these working mechanisms around a new visual premise and scene arc.

## Working examples

- [Event announcement opener](../recipes/Event%20announcement%20opener.md) combines timed text, visual emphasis, and export to a video file.
- [Diagram explainer](../recipes/Diagram%20explainer.md) combines a circle, a moving point, a guide line, and a growing curve to show one relationship over time.
- [Procedural 3D scene](../recipes/Procedural%203D%20scene.md) combines geometry, shaders, animation, and a camera view.
- [Animated data comparison](../recipes/Animated%20data%20comparison.md) combines source data, graphical marks, and transitions over time.
