# Compose time and motion

A viewer meets a video in sequence. Choose what changes, what stays, and what the viewer needs time to read. [Time and motion](../knowledge/Time%20and%20motion.md) covers how scene state relates to time; [transitions and continuity](../knowledge/Transitions%20and%20continuity.md) covers what a cut, morph, or handoff keeps from one shot to the next.

Use [visual hierarchy and composition](../knowledge/Visual%20hierarchy%20and%20composition.md) to place the focal event within each story beat, and [typography in motion](../knowledge/Typography%20in%20motion.md) when words have to be read while they move. If a frame must come out the same at a chosen timestamp every time, see [deterministic rendering](../knowledge/Deterministic%20rendering.md).

Sound can carry the same structure. [Compose sound and synchronization](Compose%20sound%20and%20synchronization.md) ties musical or other sound events to changes in the picture.
