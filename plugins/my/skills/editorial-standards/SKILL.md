Before writing, rewriting, or reviewing content for the request, read `references/editorial-standards.md` (in this skill's own directory) in full. Treat it as editorial judgment rather than a checklist. Its Scope and How to apply sections tell you which parts fit the piece at hand; not every section applies to every genre, and a rule that would damage the writing should be followed for its intent, with the departure noted in a line.

Also read `references/article-format.md` when the user requests that article structure or asks you to continue a project already using it. It does not apply to notes, documentation, correspondence, or other one-off writing.

When drafting or revising, produce prose that already reads the way the standards describe. When reviewing, identify concrete violations, explain them briefly, and propose or make compliant revisions according to the user's request. Preserve the source's meaning and factual uncertainty; do not invent evidence, attribution, or data to satisfy a rule.

If a bundled standards file cannot be read, state that clearly and do not claim compliance.
